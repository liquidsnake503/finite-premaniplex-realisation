## Abstract

This manuscript proposes a proof that every finite connected premaniplex of rank at least three is the full symmetry type graph of a finite abstract polytope. The proposed construction uses words annihilated by proper colour-deletion retractions, finite-quotient order prescriptions for independent conjugates, and a subgroup designed to preserve the path-intersection property and yield the prescribed full automorphism quotient.

The manuscript also includes an explicit nonnormal three-orbit example.
